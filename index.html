Dr. Taeyoung Kim (김태영;金泰永)

Biographical Sketch

Dr. Kim is an AI systems architect with 20+ years of engineering and research experience across silicon power/thermal modeling, EDA tooling, and production AI platforms, and a Ph.D. in Computer Science (Systems & Architecture). As a Staff AI Software Architect at Intel, he serves as Technical Lead and Security Architect Lead for Intel SuperClaw (V1.0–V2.0), Intel's open-source local AI agent reference tool, and led a 50-person client/cloud engineering organization for Intel AI Assistant Builder (V1.0–V2.9, Hybrid AI), shipping on-device GenAI releases to 10 AI PC OEM customers. His work spans LLM evaluation and cross-runtime benchmarking infrastructure (30 models across 10 silicon SKUs, 3× faster release validation), client power and thermal workload characterization, AI-assisted overclocking (Intel Core 14th Gen), and silicon power/thermal/DRAM modeling. He holds 8 patents (1 granted, 7 published), has authored 35 peer-reviewed research papers and a Springer book, and has received 8 Intel Divisional Recognition Awards (2023–2026).

He has received the M.S. degree in electrical engineering from University of Virginia, Charlottesville, VA, USA in 2012, and the Ph.D. degree in computer science from University of California, Riverside, CA, USA in 2017. His dissertation was titled "System-level Electromigration-Induced Dynamic Reliability Management." During his Ph.D. study, he received a best research award at ACM Ph.D. Forum at Design Automation Conference (DAC) in 2015 and a dissertation award from the University of California in 2017.
