Biographical Sketch

Dr. Taeyoung Kim is an AI systems architect with 20+ years of engineering and research experience across silicon power/thermal modeling, EDA tooling, and production AI platforms, holding a Ph.D. in Computer Science (Systems & Architecture). As a Staff AI Software Architect at Intel, he serves as Technical Lead and Security Architect Lead for Intel SuperClaw (V1.0–V2.0), Intel's open-source local AI agent reference tool, and led a 50-person client/cloud engineering organization for Intel AI Assistant Builder (V1.0–V2.9, Hybrid AI), shipping on-device GenAI releases to 10 AI PC OEM customers. His work spans LLM evaluation and cross-runtime benchmarking infrastructure (30 models across 10 silicon SKUs, 3× faster release validation), client power and thermal workload characterization, AI-assisted overclocking (Intel Core 14th Gen), and silicon power/thermal/DRAM modeling.

He received his M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Virginia (2012) and Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside (2017). His doctoral dissertation, System-level Electromigration-Induced Dynamic Reliability Management, earned the Best Research Award at the ACM Ph.D. Forum at DAC (2015) and the Dissertation Year Fellowship Award from UC Riverside (2016–2017).
